# WorldVoice

**Teach Once. Reach the World.**

WorldVoice is a modern course-localization company focused on helping educators, creators, academies, training teams, and organizations adapt learning experiences for global audiences.

We combine language, voice, learning content, and technology to help educational material move across languages while preserving meaning, structure, tone, terminology, and learning intent.

> **Your Courses. Every Language.**

## About WorldVoice

WorldVoice is built around one simple idea:

> **Great education should not be limited by language.**

Our focus is **course localization** — adapting educational experiences for different languages, markets, regions, and audiences.

# Course Localization

Course localization is the process of adapting learning content for a specific language and audience.

It goes beyond direct translation.

A localized learning experience may require changes to:

- Spoken language
- Written content
- Subtitles
- Terminology
- Examples
- Cultural references
- Visual elements
- Measurements
- Dates
- Currency
- Assessments
- Interface labels
- Supporting documents

WorldVoice is designed around preserving the purpose of the original course while adapting the experience for a new audience.

# Localization Workflow

A WorldVoice course-localization project may follow a structured workflow.

```text
Source Course
     ↓
Content Analysis
     ↓
Transcription
     ↓
Translation
     ↓
Localization
     ↓
Voice / Subtitles
     ↓
Learning Material Adaptation
     ↓
Quality Review
     ↓
Final Delivery
```

# Quality Standards

Localization quality involves much more than correct grammar.

WorldVoice should prioritize:

### Accuracy

The localized content should preserve the meaning of the source material.

### Clarity

Learners should be able to understand the material naturally.

### Consistency

Terminology should remain consistent throughout the course.

### Learning Intent

Localization should preserve the educational objective.

### Natural Language

Content should sound natural in the target language.

### Technical Quality

Audio, subtitle, and export quality should meet the requirements of the target platform.
